02 The problem

Every tokenised asset has to know who holds it.
At issuance, on every transfer, every day after.

Securities law, AML rules and licence conditions all ask the same question. On chain it has three answers, and all three fail the day a holder is revoked.

Answer one
Per-app KYC
Vendor contract, personal-data flow, evidence retention and rescreening, rebuilt for every asset on every chain.
Answer two
“KYC passed”
A boolean that hides who checked, what was checked, under which regime, how long ago, and whether it is still true.
Answer three
A relayer
Copy the flag to another chain and one signing key becomes the compliance authority. The trust problem moves; it does not go away.
What is missing is a policy layer the asset owns: what was checked, by whom, how recently, and whether it still stands.

03 The product

One read. The asset enforces it inside every transfer.

isVerified(wallet, policyId)
Credential
What an issuer verified. Method bitmap, assurance, regime, jurisdiction, timestamps, two commitments. Names and account numbers stay off chain.
Policy
What this asset requires. Required checks, minimum assurance, trusted issuer, regime, jurisdiction, maximum age. Frozen at birth; nobody can loosen it later.
Enforcement
Inside the transfer hook. Sender and recipient are both read. Ineligible, expired, sanctioned or revoked holders cannot send or receive.

Verify · Ethereum Sepolia
Credential in
Wallet signature, ID document, bank account, sanctions against OFAC, UN and EU source lists. Only a check that ran sets a bit. ComplianceSource emits the mark as an event.
Prove · Attestcoin
Proof, not relay
Creditcoin’s BlockProver verifies that exact Ethereum transaction inside our ASC. The worker only carries proof material. Anyone could run it; it holds no authority.
Enforce · Creditcoin CC3
Frozen policy
ProofmarkRegistry judges the mark against the asset’s policy. The tokenised note reverts on its own: RecipientNotVerified, SenderNotVerified.
Remove Attestcoin and the middle box becomes a relayer we operate. That is the trust the product exists to remove.

08 Why now · why Creditcoin

Regulation puts the holder question on the issuer.
Attestcoin makes the answer portable.

Rules
Issuer owns AML
GENIUS Act signed, MiCA in force. Korea’s FIU 2026 plan puts AML duties directly on stablecoin issuers, with freeze and burn. Gating holders is no longer optional.
Rails
Proof, not trust
Attestcoin proves Ethereum state inside Creditcoin execution today. For the first time a compliance fact can cross chains without a relayer key becoming the authority.
Demand
Repeated per asset
Every RWA, stablecoin and lending market on Creditcoin repeats the same eligibility work per asset. One frozen policy and one read replace it. Reads stay permissionless.
We do not size this top-down. Every regulated token needs the read. We sell the operations that keep it true.
